# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

First release, for the Phone & SMS watchapp 1.0.1.

- Setup in three steps: Pebble app link, permissions, call control
- Contacts, favorites with their photos, call log, and SMS and RCS conversations for the watch, pictures included
- Contacts found by voice, by name or nickname
- Calls placed and followed, with mute, audio route and hang up once call control is allowed
- Texts sent to the international form of the number, from the SIM of the conversation or the one picked in the settings
- Conversations opened in the messaging app from the watch
- Quick replies editor
- Settings with the watch menu and message sizes, what the watch launcher shows, the SIMs, About and this changelog
- Material You colors, and a launcher icon with a themed variant, named Pebble Phone Connect
- No internet permission: contacts, calls and messages only go to the watch
